# Local operation and help

Türwache is a free camera server for private households, focused on Ring cameras. The app runs on your computer, mini PC or Raspberry Pi. You connect your Ring account yourself on that device. Ring cameras still use their connection to Ring. By default, Türwache stores recordings on your device. [Ring Terms of Service for Germany](https://ring.com/de/de/terms)

## Set up the app yourself

The [Windows guide](windows.en.md) and [Raspberry Pi guide](raspberry-pi.en.md) cover installation, first startup and camera connection. See [operations and security](operations.en.md) for updates, backups and recovery.

## Setup assistance

An optional, one-time paid appointment can cover installation, storage, automatic startup and local backups. At the end, you can test a recording and a restart on your own device. You connect your Ring account yourself. Your Ring credentials, sessions and clips stay with you during this service.

Individual maintenance or recovery appointments are possible follow-up services. The app and its guides remain free for private use. The [Private Use License](../LICENSE.md) sets the terms for sharing and modifications.

Once the operator publishes appointments, you can see the available times and one-time price under **Einrichtung** on the Türwache website.
